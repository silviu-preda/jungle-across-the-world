# Albania

## Assessment

Albania has a small but identifiable contemporary underground electronic scene in Tirana with a documented jungle connection. The evidence does **not** support treating the wider Nëntokë scene as a jungle scene: much of the material examined is techno, tekno, breakbeat or experimental electronic music. The qualifying jungle case established during this research is 010010.

## Labels

### Nëntokë Recordings
**Activity:** 2023–present  
**Location:** Tirana, Albania

Nëntokë Recordings is part of the Tirana underground electronic movement NËNTOKË. Its catalogue is broader than jungle, encompassing techno, breaks and other electronic styles, but it has a documented jungle/D&B connection through 010010 and jungle-oriented programming. It should therefore be understood as an **Albanian underground electronic label/community with a jungle component**, rather than simply a jungle label.

## Artists

### 010010
**Activity:** 2022–present  
**Location:** Tirana, Albania

010010 is a Tirana-based DJ/producer associated with Nëntokë Recordings and Soundsystem. The artist has a documented jungle track, **“Hardcore Jungle”**, which satisfies the project's minimum requirement for artist inclusion. The track has a pronounced techno/hardcore character rather than presenting as conventional straight-ahead jungle; this is an example of the porous genre boundary used by the project.

The *Hardcore Jungle* release also contains **“Dready”**, which the project owner considers a particularly strong track. Other material in the artist's catalogue includes hardtek and D&B-adjacent material that does not necessarily qualify as jungle. The important point for inclusion is that the artist has demonstrably produced at least one track judged to be jungle.

## Research exclusions / candidates

A number of other Tirana artists were investigated because of their association with Nëntokë, NNTK001, or jungle/D&B-labelled events. They were **not included as qualifying artists** because the research did not establish an individual jungle track meeting the project's threshold, or because the owner's listening assessment placed the material outside jungle.

### NNTK001

The NNTK001 compilation was listened to as a scene-mapping exercise. The project owner's assessment is that the release is predominantly **techno/tekno and adjacent experimental electronic music**, not jungle or meaningfully jungle-adjacent. “Rock Spot” by Rumble Project contains chopped Amen material and has an IDM/experimental character, but the Amen usage alone was judged insufficient to classify it as jungle or jungle-adjacent for this project.

Consequently, NNTK001 is **not treated as a jungle compilation** and its artists are not automatically promoted to qualifying jungle artists.

### Treg Elektronik

Treg Elektronik is a Tirana-based electronic artist active from at least 2024, with connections to both Nëntokë and Teknik Tirana and a body of work centred on techno/tekno and related electronic styles. The artist was investigated as a possible jungle candidate but, after listening, was **not found to meet the project's jungle criterion**.

### ZeroCase / Zer0case

ZeroCase is associated with Tirana's underground scene and has documented exposure to hardcore, tekno, electro, D&B and jungle. However, no individual jungle track was established to the project's satisfaction during this research. **Not included.**

### D-Law and KRIΣTIΛN

Both appeared in Nëntokë programming advertised around Electro, Drum & Bass and Jungle. Event participation alone was insufficient to satisfy the project's requirement for an individual jungle track. **Not included pending stronger evidence.**

## Scene conclusion

The Albanian evidence currently supports a **small Tirana-based jungle presence embedded within a much broader underground electronic/techno ecosystem**, rather than a distinct large-scale national jungle scene. Nëntokë provides an important piece of infrastructure, while 010010 is the clearly established qualifying jungle artist identified in this pass.

The classification deliberately does not turn every breakbeat, D&B, Amen-containing or techno-adjacent track into jungle. The owner's listening judgement is decisive where genre boundaries are ambiguous.
