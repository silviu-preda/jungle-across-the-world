# Argentina

## Labels

### Killerdrumz Records

**Documented activity:** 2017–present  
**Operation:** Buenos Aires, Argentina / Latin America

Killerdrumz Records is an independent label connected to the Killerdrumz community: a dark drum & bass, hardcore, crossbreed and breakcore network operating from Buenos Aires while working across Latin America.

The label’s catalogue is a genuinely relevant porous-boundary case, with releases spanning breakcore, crossbreed, dark D&B, hardcore and related broken-beat forms. Its Bandcamp remains active, including the *Breakcore Never Die Compilation* in January 2026.

The Argentina allocation rests on the collective’s documented Buenos Aires activity, from its 2010 *Therapy Sessions* event to a forthcoming Killerdrumz co-presented party in the city. This is not merely an imported label or a one-off booking.

**Date:** 11 September 2026
