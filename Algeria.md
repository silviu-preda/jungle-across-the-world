# Algeria

## Artists

### Idriss D

**Relevant jungle activity:** 2020–present  
**Algerian connection:** formative DJ activity in Algiers from 1998; later based in Italy

Idriss D began DJing while living in Algeria in 1998, after entering the club culture of Algiers and playing his first set there. His later career developed in Italy, but his jungle and D&B roots are explicitly tied to that Algerian beginning.

**“Lunatic”** (2020) is his first jungle project: a genuinely hectic, early-90s-informed jungle track rather than a loose database tag. It is the essential entry point for this project.

**“Lunatic 2”** (2023) continues the breakbeat/jungle/D&B line in a more diffuse form. It remains relevant, but *Lunatic* is the stronger and more definitive inclusion.
