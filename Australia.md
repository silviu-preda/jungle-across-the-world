# Australia

Australia is a high-load country for this project. The file is therefore organized by **regional ecosystem and label/collective**, with artists nested beneath the discovery route that surfaced them. This is an initial, non-exhaustive map.

Artist allocation follows the project guardrails: qualifying activity in the Australian scene matters more than birthplace, and releasing through a foreign label does not remove an artist from Australia. Conversely, an overseas artist on an Australian label does not automatically become an Australian-scene artist.

## Core ecosystems

### Newcastle

#### Bloody Fist Records

**Relevant activity:** 1994–2004, with archival reissues afterward  
**Role:** Core label

Bloody Fist qualifies outright. Its abrasive catalogue spans hardcore, speedcore, breakcore and industrial music, but it also contains substantial jungle-bearing material rather than merely touching the genre at the edges.

**Qualifying artists and evidence**

- **Syndicate** — a direct historical entry from the Newcastle/Bloody Fist ecosystem. The listening review confirmed the group's jungle relevance.
- **Epsilon** — *Christ as Bath Toy* is a useful example of the catalogue's jungle/breakcore junction. It supports Bloody Fist's label entry; Epsilon's own country allocation should remain independently sourced.

Bloody Fist is one of the clearest Australian discoveries in this project, not a contextual footnote.

#### Hardline Rekordingz / Hardline Rekz Digital

**Relevant activity:** 2000s–present  
**Role:** Core label at the jungle/hardcore/breakcore junction

Hardline repeatedly crosses hardcore, breakcore, drum and bass and jungle. Accepted evidence includes *The Subtle Arts of Murder & Persuasion* series, *Broke / Mindjack VIP*, *Hardline 13 – Viva La Revolucion*, Arkon's *Rapture*, and *Drum Embrace*. Part 1 of *The Subtle Arts* is the stronger jungle document, although the available Bandcamp master has badly distorted Amen breaks. *Breakology* is excluded.

**Qualifying artists**

- **Paulblackout** — central to the later Hardline story. *Hardline 13 – Viva La Revolucion* sits at the accepted D&B/jungle junction, while *Drum Embrace* adds an IDM/breaks fusion.
- **Arkon** — Perth-associated Australian artist in the Hardline circle. *Rapture* is the clearest direct Amen-led qualification found so far.

### Sydney

#### Jungle Massive Australia

**Relevant activity:** Mid-1990s onward  
**Role:** Foundational collective and scene infrastructure

Jungle Massive Australia (JMA) is essential historical infrastructure. DJ Popeye's *Jungle Massive Australia Vol. 1* (1995) and *This Is Jungle: JMA Vol. II* (1996) are strong period documents. They are DJ tapes/mixes, not original productions, and are treated accordingly.

**Associated artists**

- **DJ Popeye** — selector and historical scene figure documented through the JMA tapes. The tapes establish circulation and scene activity rather than a production catalogue.
- **DJ Speaks** — early JMA participant who later became one half of Tactical Aspect.
- **Tactical Aspect** — the duo of DJ Speaks and Ruff Tactics. The project is firmly jungle and connects the formative Sydney network to later Australian production.

Archived forums, surviving tape uploads and old tracklists are particularly important here because conventional streaming metadata is incomplete.

### Melbourne

#### Rising Lantern Records

**Relevant activity:** 2008–present  
**Role:** Core label and discovery hub

Rising Lantern is run by brothers MshCode and Time Travel. It is a primary route into Melbourne's dubwise jungle network.

**Qualifying artists**

- **MshCode** — co-runs the label. *Silver Dub – The Remixes* was a strong listening result and helps establish the dub/jungle junction.
- **Time Travel** — co-runs the label and participates across the Rising Lantern, Bitwise and Echo Chamber networks.

**Strong catalogue leads still needing independent artist allocation**

- **Rich Tones** — especially strong in the listening review.
- **Stemfilth** — additional individual trail through *Intensity* and *Three Spots*.
- **Civilian Flexibility** — appears in the *Dub Plate Specials* series.
- **Visible Sound** — remains a worthwhile lead, but must not be identified as Alistair Brown without better evidence.
- **Subnet43, OneSec, SpinFX, C:1 and Strangenotes** — part of the wider roster/discovery trail; roster appearance alone is not enough for Australian allocation.

#### Bitwise

**Relevant activity:** 2010s–present  
**Role:** Core label

Bitwise is one of the strongest discoveries from the Australian research. Confirmed listening highlights include *Silver Dub – The Remixes*, Subnet43's *The Empirical EP*, and especially *The Mutant Limit EP*, whose clean, energetic Amen chopping invited comparison with Tim Reaper.

Time Travel and MshCode connect Bitwise to the wider Melbourne ecosystem. Subnet43 and Mutant Limit are firm musical discoveries, but their individual Australian-scene allocation still needs independent provenance.

#### Echo Chamber Sound / Run It Red

**Relevant activity:** 2008–present  
**Role:** Core label, soundsystem and record-shop network

Echo Chamber Sound is a Melbourne institution with New Zealand roots and a deliberately trans-Tasman catalogue. Run It Red is its jungle-focused subsidiary and is the clearest genre-specific arm.

**Qualifying Australian-scene artists**

- **LQ** — New Zealand-born but established in Melbourne as a producer, DJ, label operator and soundsystem figure. The listening review confirmed definite jungle in his catalogue.
- **Kloke** — British-born and Melbourne-based. His substantial jungle work, including collaborations with Tim Reaper, qualifies through sustained Australian-scene activity.
- **Dizz1** — Melbourne artist whose *In the Zone EP* includes explicit junglist work.

**Catalogue evidence and cross-border distinctions**

- **Zeldin** — *As Time Goes By* passed the jungle listening test; individual provenance still needs resolution before country allocation.
- **Shadow Club** and **Midnight Dubs** — useful catalogue evidence, but New Zealand-scene artists rather than Australian allocations.
- **Sekkleman** — predominantly dub for this project's purpose and not currently qualified as an artist entry.

#### Pugilist and cross-label activity

**Relevant activity:** 2010s–present  
**Role:** Core Melbourne-based artist operating across labels

Pugilist is a Scottish/Kiwi producer operating from Melbourne. His qualifying jungle material is scattered across labels rather than confined to a single local imprint.

*Waveforms 19–20* is the strongest entry found so far: both tracks use mid-1990s break architecture, with *Waveform 20* providing a direct Amen workout. *Onyx* is another major result. The Pugilist & Tamen *Source* EP supplies a further jungle lane. *Looking Out* is good but sits slightly outside jungle.

Ruff Kutz is presented as cross-border release infrastructure, not reassigned to Australia merely because it releases Pugilist.

#### Detrimental Audio

**Relevant activity:** 2010s–present  
**Role:** Australian-origin, later cross-border label

Detrimental Audio was established in Melbourne and is now based in London. Both parts of that history matter: it belongs in the Australian-origin story without being falsely described as a currently Australian label.

**Associated artists and leads**

- **Tactical Aspect** — confirmed Australian-scene jungle act.
- **Tokyo Noir** — Melbourne producer whose *Dopamine* on Detrimental passed the listening test and supplies the qualifying jungle evidence. The Inna Riddim release *The Feelin' / Keep the Vibes Alive* was rejected as non-qualifying.
- **Pickleman** — musically strong and has a Tim Reaper connection, but personal Australian provenance remains unresolved.

### Perth and Western Australia

#### Greg Packer / Interphase

**Relevant activity:** 1990s–present  
**Role:** Core artist; Interphase is a contextual historical imprint

Greg Packer is a substantial Perth jungle/drum-and-bass figure. *Terrorschizm (Jungle Mix)* with MC Assassin is the strongest original-production anchor found in this pass. *Gunshot Riddim* leans further toward drum and bass, while later releases are generally less decisive for this project's narrower purpose. His 1990s DJ recordings document early local jungle circulation but are kept distinct from original productions.

Interphase is retained as a brief historical note attached to Packer. Its Perth connection is clear, but the accessible catalogue evidence found so far is too limited for a full label profile.

#### Third Degree

**Relevant activity:** 2020s–present  
**Role:** Core contemporary Perth/Boorloo label

Third Degree qualifies through *HAZMAT: Hazardous Materials Vol. 002*, which was ear-checked as a substantially jungle release rather than a compilation with one accidental outlier. The first *HAZMAT* volume is less consistent for this project.

**Qualifying artist**

- **Ghost Snares** — an Australian artist with documented Perth-scene activity. *Re-Creation* on *HAZMAT Vol. 002* supplied the local label connection, while the later *True Love* and *For Real / Dream Jungle* releases on Tru Love Records show that the jungle work is sustained beyond one compilation appearance.

**Catalogue evidence and cross-border distinctions**

- **Sterlo** — *Sunblush* passed the listening test, but Sterlo is Sheffield-based. The track supports Third Degree's label qualification without turning Sterlo into an Australian artist.
- R-15+'s *FEEL U* and Calm Stiege's *Mood Eye* were rejected. RESY is Perth-based but did not provide enough jungle evidence to qualify as an artist entry.

#### Little Rascal Records

**Relevant activity:** Contemporary  
**Role:** Qualifying broad-spectrum Perth label

Little Rascal is not a jungle-first label, but repeated qualifying material across its catalogue establishes a real, narrowly described jungle connection.

**Qualifying artist**

- **DJ Jools** — *Haunted* on *Rolling Rascals Vol. 1* is definite jungle. *High Rolling EP* works at the jungle/D&B junction, while *Angular* on *Intelligence EP* is junglish within a release whose broader feel is drum and bass.

**Supporting catalogue evidence**

- **Jerry Mane** — *Jahgular Jungle* qualifies as a track, but the wider catalogue does not justify consolidating Jerry Mane as a jungle artist.
- **Maxx Honey** — *D vs N* is junglish and supports the label case, but does not yet justify a separate artist entry.

#### BUEX

**Relevant activity:** Contemporary  
**Role:** Direct Australian-scene artist with cross-border releases

BUEX remains relevant to Australia even when released through foreign labels. *Form* appeared on the UK label Omni Music, while *Eat the Bangers Vol. 1* supplied the stronger listening case for inclusion. Omni remains a UK label; BUEX remains attached to the Australian scene.

#### ENDE Records Australia

**Relevant activity:** 2014–present  
**Role:** Qualifying porous-boundary label

ENDE is predominantly a breakcore/hardcore/IDM/noise label, but its catalogue contains enough genuine jungle to qualify at label level.

Enja Bergman's *Atmospheric Jungle* was found to be Amen jungle throughout, and 3 Past 3 supplied another strong jungle/breakcore result. Both artists remain allocated outside Australia where their own provenance requires it: they establish ENDE's qualifying catalogue, not Australian artist status.

### Direct Australian artist outside a resolved label cluster

#### 12bit Jungle Out There

**Relevant activity:** Contemporary  
**Location:** Australia

12bit Jungle Out There is a direct jungle entry. The Amiga/12-bit production identity is part of the project's character rather than a reason to file the work only as breakcore. A more precise local and label connection remains to be documented.

## Context and footnotes

These entries matter to the surrounding ecosystem, but they are intentionally not presented as peers of the core labels above.

- **8-Bit Recordings — Perth:** primarily breakcore, glitch, IDM and noise. Holt's *Nocturne* is the likeliest qualifying track found so far, while Deadcode's *The Dirty Truth* contains worthwhile chopped-break material. Retain as a contextual label mention.
- **System Corrupt / SY:CO:** a breakcore, noise, speedcore and hardcore network with occasional jungle fusion. Contextual scene history, not a jungle-label claim.
- **Anomaly Digital:** a future-jungle/hardcore-breaks label whose catalogue proved less decisive than its descriptions. Kranky & Lethal were too tangential; the Time Travel connection warrants only supporting context.
- **Inna Riddim — Sydney:** a broad bass/D&B label rather than a jungle imprint. Aural Blueprint's *Reminisce* passed as a jungle/D&B crossover and supports a narrow contextual label mention. Anomie's *What You Gonna Do About It* remains borderline; Tokyo Noir's *The Feelin' / Keep the Vibes Alive* and *Hijack* featuring Slice MC were rejected.
- **Golden Orb Records — Brisbane:** an atmospheric/liquid D&B label, not a jungle label. The only qualifying evidence found is Shebuzzz's *In Infinity*, whose aggressive Amen drop passed the listening test. Shebuzzz's own country allocation is unresolved. Golden Orb is therefore a narrow footnote, not a core ecosystem entry.
- **Best Effort:** not a jungle label. Nutcase & Papachubba and a Hugh B release justify only light artist-level follow-up.
- **Xian:** important Melbourne breakcore history, but the reviewed tracks were not pure jungle. *Cold Crash Breakcore* came closest, followed by *Dem*.
- **Atom 1 / Soundboy Melbourne:** *Imminent* documents historical Melbourne techstep/drum and bass, not a jungle anchor.

## Open research ledger

The following listening and provenance work is preserved without promoting unresolved material into the ecosystem map:

- **Big Dog Records / Adelaide:** *MPK Presents: Archive – A Collection of Original Adelaide Drum & Bass* has been located as a downloadable archive. Full listening is deferred until the files can be auditioned; Big Dog is not yet a confirmed entry.
- **B&H catalogue:** *BotB 2* was found jungle-focused; Nickname's *Groove 4* has an old-school jungle sound; *Deep into the Rave* is substantially jungle, including Noneohone's *CARLISLE* and MOOD$' *Forgotten*; SAV/Blank's *Dune Rider* is liquid jungle; Knuckle's *Trick* and Myledo's *Freddy No* also passed listening. Exact label identity and Australian provenance still need resolution.
- **XTR catalogue:** XTR001 did not qualify. Knuckle's *Manifold* from *Vamos EP* is definite jungle, *Trick* also works, and Oi Ocha's *Jungle Informer EP* is relevant. Possible People's material was judged drum and bass. Label identity and artist provenance still need documentation.
- **Rising Lantern artist provenance:** Rich Tones, Stemfilth, Visible Sound and Civilian Flexibility require individual allocation work.
- **Bitwise artist provenance:** Mutant Limit and Subnet43 are firmly qualified musically but not yet individually allocated.
- **Pickleman:** qualifies musically; Australian scene provenance remains unresolved.

## Excluded or deprioritized in this pass

Royalston, Evil Eye Recordings, CC:White, *Breakology*, Possible People, Sekkleman, Eko-system / Mondowun's *Frequent Flyer EP*, and Gorilla Tactics Music as a label. The reviewed *Soul Ryderz (Jungle Don Mix)* is drum and bass rather than jungle; MC Assassin remains relevant through Greg Packer's qualifying *Terrorschizm (Jungle Mix)*.
