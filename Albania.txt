ALBANIA
=======

Assessment
----------
Albania has a small but clearly identifiable contemporary jungle / drum & bass ecosystem centred on Tirana. The evidence points to an underground community around Nëntokë and related venues/events rather than a large, historically established national jungle scene.

LABELS
------

Nëntokë Recordings
Activity: 2023–present (recording activity; wider NËNTOKË movement established 2020)
Location: Tirana, Albania

Nëntokë Recordings is part of the Tirana underground electronic scene and is associated with NËNTOKË, described by the organisation itself as an underground movement established in 2020. Its catalogue and activities include drum & bass, breaks and jungle-adjacent material. The label's Bandcamp identifies it as based in Tirana, while its first documented release, 010010's Streat No-ledge, appeared in January 2023 and is tagged drum & bass / DnB. Nëntokë also organised jungle/D&B programming in Tirana, including a 2024 event explicitly promoted around Electro, Drum & Bass and Jungle. [1][2][3]

ARTISTS
-------

010010
Activity: 2023–present
Location: Tirana, Albania

010010 is a Tirana-based producer/DJ and founder of Nëntokë Recordings/Soundsystem. The artist's own Bandcamp catalogue explicitly includes a track titled "Hardcore Jungle", while another 2023 release is tagged jungle. Nëntokë describes 010010 as a DJ working in Drum & Bass and identifies the project as central to its local underground community. 010010 therefore meets the project's explicit requirement that an artist have at least one jungle track in their portfolio. [2][4][5]

ZeroCase (Dejvid Sherri / Zer0case)
Activity: 2024–present
Location: Albania / Tirana

ZeroCase emerged from Tirana's underground scene and is described by Resident Advisor as exploring hardcore, tekno, electro, DnB and jungle in sets and productions. His first recorded Resident Advisor event is in 2024, and subsequent appearances include Nëntokë events alongside 010010. This establishes both a qualifying jungle connection and participation in the Albanian/Tirana ecosystem. [6]

NOT INCLUDED YET
----------------

D-Law, DJ Ferro, Chris (DRK), Kristian and other artists appearing on Albanian jungle/D&B event lineups were not added as qualifying artist entries solely on the basis of those appearances. Under the guardrails, each artist needs identifiable evidence of at least one jungle track in their portfolio. They remain candidates for further research.

SCENE NOTES
-----------

The strongest evidence currently points to Tirana rather than a geographically broad Albanian scene. NËNTOKË describes itself as a community-driven underground platform and soundsystem, while its events and releases provide a continuing local infrastructure around bass music. A 2024 Nëntokë event at Technosphere explicitly advertised Electro, Drum & Bass and Jungle, and a November 2025 Drum N'Bass Night at the same venue was explicitly categorised as Drum & Bass and Jungle. [1][3][7]
