# Andorra

## Assessment

Andorra has a small contemporary jungle/breakcore presence through **timesgone**.

## Artists

### timesgone

**Activity:** 2024–present  
**Location:** Andorra

timesgone is an Andorra-based project with a cross-genre catalogue spanning jungle, D&B, breakcore, techno and house.

The qualifying case is **“i’m the one in love ft. DNA”** (2025): an Amen-led breakcore track with clear jungle DNA. It qualifies timesgone under the project’s porous genre boundary.

The 2024 *overdose* release is a particularly strong entry point. Later work moves more freely across genres; playlist inclusion should therefore stay selective rather than treating the whole catalogue as jungle.
