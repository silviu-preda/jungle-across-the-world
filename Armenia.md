# Armenia

## Artists

### Paluca

**Relevant jungle activity:** 2009–present  
**Current location:** Gyumri, Armenia

Paluca is the jungle/drumfunk project of Aleksey Stepanov. Stepanov’s own biography dates the project to 2009 and describes a dark jungle/drumfunk practice with releases on more than ten labels across the UK, US, Russia and elsewhere. His current Bandcamp profile places Paluca in Gyumri.

This is a substantial, continuing jungle practice rather than a one-track claim. The project's listening assessment identifies recurring, properly formed jungle in the catalogue: the breaks are not a constant Amen barrage, but the chopping is precise and deeply musical.

A 2021 radio profile described Stepanov as Moscow-based. This entry therefore documents his current Armenia-based chapter; it does not retroactively assign his entire career to Armenia.

### Dave N.A. / Dave Nyx Aether

**Relevant jungle activity:** 2019–present  
**Location:** Yerevan, Armenia

Dave N.A. is a Yerevan-based producer working across jungle, breakbeat, D&B, techno and experimental electronic music. His early local releases include directly jungle-tagged material, while his 2026 *Sky City* EP is explicitly described by its label as rooted in jungle and breakbeat.

His place here is not a genre-tag technicality. The project's listening assessment considers *Sky City* a particularly strong release, and Dave's work carries jungle technique and history into a broader, carefully built breakbeat language.

## Labels

### Medium Raw Records

**Established:** 2019  
**Location:** Yerevan, Armenia

Medium Raw Records is the small Yerevan imprint run by Dave N.A. and MEROUJ. Its documented release period runs from 2019 to 2021, with work by Dave, MEROUJ and YOZEMAL.

It belongs here specifically as part of Dave N.A.'s local infrastructure: Dave’s Medium Raw releases include direct jungle material. The label is not being presented as a large or continually active national jungle catalogue.

## Recent relevant events

### Drum & Bass Rave

**Date:** 18–19 April 2026  
**Location:** Stop Club, Yerevan

A full-night local D&B event with jungle sets from Andmiro and Mugen Smoll alongside halftime, neurofunk, techstep, liquid and deep D&B. Its invitation put people around the DJ, with no boundary between artist and ravers: unusually direct evidence of the DJ–crowd relationship central to this project.

### DiliRock 2026 — Experimental Stage

**Dates:** 28–30 August 2026  
**Location:** Dilijan, Armenia

The current edition of this independent forest festival programmes jungle, breaks and breakcore on its Experimental Stage, including bax4es (jungle / breaks / breakcore), Sh∆ntr//v (breakcore) and andrey rylov (micro-jungle / deconstructed breaks). It is a broad arts festival, but its programme gives the connected genres a real contemporary place in Armenia’s live ecology.
